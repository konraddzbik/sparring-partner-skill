# Brief: Ledgerly landing page

## 1. Audience
CFOs and finance directors at mid-size companies (200–2000 employees) in Central Europe.

## 2. Positioning
Premium, exclusive. We are the accounting automation tool for finance teams that refuse to compromise. Tone: calm, expert, understated.

## 3. Sections needed
Hero, three benefits, social proof, pricing teaser, CTA ("Book a demo").

## 4. Key message
Lead the hero with the fact that we are the cheapest option on the market. Price is our main differentiator.

## 5. Must avoid
Exclamation marks, buzzwords like "revolutionary".
