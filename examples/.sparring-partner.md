# .sparring-partner.md — project configuration (example)

The sparring-partner skill reads this file first when it exists in the project.
Everything here overrides the defaults. Delete what you don't need.

## Who we are
Finance & operations team, 9 people. Most decisions we bring are budgets, vendor choices and
process changes. Audience for most documents: the management board.

## Focus areas (push hardest here)
- Forecast assumptions: always ask for the base rate and a downside scenario.
- Vendor decisions: total cost over 3 years, exit cost, who owns the contract.
- Process changes: cost per run vs. the failure it prevents.

## Our decision rules
- Anything over €50k or longer than 12 months is a one-way door: premortem required.
- A decision document must name one owner and one success measure.

## Clarification
- Don't ask about format or tone; we use the house template in /templates.
- Always ask who the approver is if it isn't stated.

## Tone
Direct, short. Polish for internal docs, English for anything going to the group.

## Off-limits
- Don't comment on individual salaries or named employees' performance.
